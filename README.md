## AstroVerse

A TESS light curve goes in. The CNN and the transformer read the flux. The physics network reads twelve stellar columns and never receives the light curve. The gate is a softmax over those three confidences. Shruti Srivastava and Taher Babat built this with me at MIT World Peace University.

[mahik504/AstroVerse](https://github.com/mahik504/AstroVerse)

Catalog-scale precision and recall are the next measurement. They are not in this release.

## XplainAI

Explainable research workspace from the IIIT Pune hackathon finals. `claim_extractor.py` splits an answer into sentences. A sentence stays unverified until two content words also appear in an evidence chunk. The web app can show those claims on a 2D or 3D graph.
